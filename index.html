Score Interpretation Guide

0 – 10 Low — Few autistic traits
11 – 21 Average — Within typical range (mean ≈ 16.4)
22 – 25 Above Average — Elevated autistic traits
26 – 31 Borderline — Consider professional assessment
32 – 50 Significant — Clinically significant autistic traits

Reference

Baron-Cohen, S., Wheelwright, S., Skinner, R., Martin, J., & Clubley, E. (2001). The Autism-Spectrum Quotient (AQ): Evidence from Asperger Syndrome/High-Functioning Autism, Males and Females, Scientists and Mathematicians. Journal of Autism and Developmental Disorders, 31, 5–17.

This questionnaire is a screening instrument only. It is not intended to provide a clinical diagnosis. A high score does not mean you have autism — only a qualified professional can make that determination. If you are experiencing distress, please seek support from a licensed clinician.
